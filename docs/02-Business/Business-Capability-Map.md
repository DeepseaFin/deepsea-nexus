## Purpose

The Business Capability Map defines every capability required to operate Deepsea Nexus.

Capabilities represent enduring business functions.

Software modules, workflows, APIs and AI agents exist to support these capabilities.

# Capability Principles

Every capability must define:

• Business Owner

• Purpose

• Inputs

• Outputs

• Workflow

• KPIs

• Decision Engines

• AI Support

• APIs

• Reports

• Audit Requirements

Every capability follows the same structure.

This consistency enables scalability and governance.
