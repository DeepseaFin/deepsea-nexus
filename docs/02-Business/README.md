# Purpose

Business Architecture defines how Deepsea Nexus operates as an institutional private credit company.

Technology exists to support the business.

The business never exists to support technology.

Every module within DNOS must correspond to a real business capability.

---

# Business Model

Deepsea Nexus originates, evaluates, funds, monitors and manages institutional private credit transactions.

Initially the platform supports:

• Receivables Finance

• Forfaiting

• Invoice Discounting

• Supply Chain Finance

Future expansion includes:

• Structured Credit

• Trade Finance

• Bond Distribution

• Institutional Debt Investments

---

# Business Operating Model

Deepsea operates through specialised business functions.

Each function owns a clearly defined responsibility.

No responsibility overlaps.

Every decision has an owner.

# Business Philosophy

Every department exists for one reason.

To improve investment decisions.

Departments do not compete.

Departments collaborate through workflows.

# The Deal Lifecycle

Every Deal progresses through a structured lifecycle.

Opportunity

↓

Client Qualification

↓

Deal Intake

↓

Eligibility

↓

Pricing

↓

Risk

↓

Constitution

↓

Investment Committee

↓

Documentation

↓

Funding

↓

Collections

↓

Settlement

↓

No Deal may bypass a required stage without authorised approval.

---

# Ownership Model

Every Deal has exactly one owner at every stage.

Ownership transfers automatically as workflow progresses.

Every ownership change is recorded.

Every decision remains auditable.
