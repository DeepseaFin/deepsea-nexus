# ATLAS Product Requirements Document - Version 1.0

## 1. Executive Summary
ATLAS Version 1.0 establishes a unified business workflow for originating, assessing, approving, and executing receivables financing transactions with institutional discipline and transparency.

Version 1.0 is designed to replace fragmented, manual, and email-driven decision cycles with a structured operating model that improves speed, consistency, and control across front office, risk, legal, and operations.

This document defines the product outcomes, scope boundaries, acceptance criteria, and release intent for Version 1.0. It is a living document and will evolve with governance decisions and release learnings.

## 2. Product Vision
ATLAS is the institutional decision-and-execution workspace for receivables financing.

Its vision is to unify coverage, risk, legal, operations, and approval stakeholders around one coherent transaction lifecycle, from first enquiry through post-funding monitoring, with explainable intelligence and auditable decisions.

Version 1.0 realizes the first complete institutional baseline by delivering:
- A guided, end-to-end business workflow
- A shared transaction record across functions
- Structured recommendation and approval readiness
- Operational readiness for funding and portfolio handoff

## 3. Business Problem
Receivables financing workflows are often constrained by:
- Disconnected tools and inconsistent handoffs across teams
- Incomplete transaction information at critical decision points
- Slow cycle times from repeated manual validation
- Limited explainability of recommendations and decisions
- Weak audit traceability for policy and approval governance
- Difficulty scaling consistent quality across increased deal volumes

These conditions create avoidable operational risk, reduced throughput, and uneven client experience.

ATLAS Version 1.0 addresses these challenges by standardizing the business journey, enforcing capability-level ownership, and enabling transparent decision progression.

## 4. Target Users

### Relationship Manager
- Owns client engagement, deal origination, and commercial progression
- Requires a guided workspace to capture and advance transactions with clear readiness signals

### Credit Analyst
- Owns credit assessment inputs to recommendation and approval
- Requires consistent visibility into transaction quality, risk posture, and conditions

### Legal
- Owns legal sufficiency and enforceability readiness
- Requires clear tracking of legal conditions and closure requirements

### Operations
- Owns execution readiness, post-approval control closure, and funding handoff
- Requires a definitive source of transaction status and condition completion

### Investment Committee
- Owns high-stakes approval decisions and exception governance
- Requires structured recommendation, confidence context, and documented rationale

### Management
- Owns performance, policy effectiveness, and institutional control posture
- Requires clear operational and portfolio visibility across pipeline and outcomes

## 6. Functional Scope (Version 1.0)
Version 1.0 includes the following functional capabilities.

### 6.1 Origination and Qualification
- Guided transaction workspace for Relationship Managers
- Client qualification and profile capture
- Counterparty qualification and exposure context
- Transaction creation and lifecycle stage progression

### 6.2 Commercial Structuring
- Commercial terms definition and review
- Funding requirement and return expectation visibility
- Structured preparation for recommendation readiness

### 6.3 Evidence and Intelligence
- Evidence collection and transaction completeness tracking
- Intelligence workspace for multi-perspective assessment context
- Consolidated confidence and recommendation support signals

### 6.4 Recommendation and Decision Readiness
- Structured recommendation workflow
- Condition and blocker visibility
- Transition from assessment to approval-ready state

### 6.5 Term Sheet and Approval Preparation
- Term sheet stage management for Version 1.0 business flow continuity
- Approval stage progression with institutional decision controls

### 6.6 Execution Handoff and Funding Readiness
- Funding readiness stage in the lifecycle
- Controlled handoff posture to operations after approval outcomes

### 6.7 Monitoring Baseline
- Monitoring stage inclusion in Version 1.0 journey baseline
- Foundational post-funding visibility for institutional continuity

### 6.8 Governance Baseline
- Policy alignment posture for decision progression
- Audit-conscious transaction lifecycle and decision traceability

## 7. Out of Scope (Deferred to Version 2.0)
The following are explicitly out of scope for Version 1.0:
- Advanced predictive portfolio analytics and automated dynamic limit optimization
- Fully autonomous recommendation generation without human oversight
- End-to-end external ecosystem orchestration across third-party providers
- Comprehensive multi-jurisdiction legal automation and clause self-remediation
- Automated exception adjudication without committee decisioning
- Real-time enterprise-wide optimization across treasury, pricing, and risk appetite
- Full institutional memory intelligence loop with closed-loop learning automation
- Advanced scenario simulation studio for complex stress and restructuring strategies
- Automated covenant surveillance with autonomous remediation workflows
- Hyper-personalized client opportunity generation and cross-product expansion intelligence

## 8. Success Criteria (Version 1.0)
Version 1.0 will be accepted when the following measurable criteria are met.

### 8.1 Workflow Completion and Coverage
- 100% of in-scope receivables transactions can be progressed through all defined Version 1.0 stages
- 100% of mandatory stage transitions are captured within ATLAS workflow state

### 8.2 Operational Throughput
- Median transaction progression time from creation to approval readiness improves by at least 30% versus pre-ATLAS baseline
- At least 90% of transactions meet internal target turnaround windows for recommendation readiness

### 8.3 Quality and Consistency
- At least 95% of transactions reach approval stage with complete mandatory information
- Rework caused by missing critical information at approval stage is reduced by at least 40%

### 8.4 Decision Transparency
- 100% of approval-bound transactions include recommendation context and documented conditions
- 100% of approval decisions include recorded rationale and outcome status

### 8.5 Control and Governance
- 100% of key lifecycle actions are traceable for audit review
- Policy-aligned progression adherence remains above 95% for in-scope transactions

### 8.6 User Adoption
- At least 85% active usage across designated user groups within 90 days of launch
- User satisfaction for workflow clarity and usability averages at least 4.0/5

## 9. Non Functional Requirements

### 9.1 Performance
- Workflow interactions should support responsive day-to-day operations at institutional usage volumes
- Core stage navigation and transaction state updates should remain within acceptable business interaction tolerances

### 9.2 Security
- Access to transaction information must be role-appropriate and institutionally governed
- Sensitive business information must be handled according to internal security and confidentiality policies

### 9.3 Explainability
- Recommendation and decision stages must provide clear, business-readable rationale context
- Decision posture should be interpretable by business and governance stakeholders without technical mediation

### 9.4 Auditability
- Transaction progression, key actions, and decision outcomes must be traceable and reviewable
- Audit records must support internal control, risk governance, and regulatory review expectations

### 9.5 Usability
- User journey must remain clear, stage-oriented, and operationally intuitive for target functions
- The product must support multi-role collaboration without ambiguity of ownership or status
