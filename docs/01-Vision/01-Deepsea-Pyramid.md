# The Deepsea Pyramid

## Purpose

The Deepsea Pyramid defines the hierarchy that governs every decision, policy, workflow and software implementation within Deepsea Nexus.

Every lower layer must remain consistent with the layer above it.

---

# Level 1 — Constitution

The Constitution defines why Deepsea exists.

It establishes the institution's purpose, values, philosophy and long-term direction.

The Constitution is the highest governing document.

---

# Level 2 — Credit Doctrines

Credit Doctrines describe how Deepsea thinks.

They are timeless principles that guide judgement.

Doctrines rarely change.

---

# Level 3 — Policies

Policies translate doctrines into practical business rules.

Policies may evolve as regulations, products and markets change.

---

# Level 4 — Standard Operating Procedures

SOPs define how work is executed consistently.

They describe operational responsibilities, approvals and workflows.

---

# Level 5 — AI Knowledge Base

The AI Knowledge Base captures Deepsea's institutional knowledge.

It contains products, industries, jurisdictions, case studies, fraud patterns, legal precedents and lessons learned.

ATLAS learns from this layer.

---

# Level 6 — Decision Orchestrator

The Decision Orchestrator coordinates every intelligence engine.

It combines evidence from multiple sources into a single explainable recommendation.

---

# Level 7 — Software

ATLAS is the software implementation of Deepsea's institutional knowledge.

Software is expected to evolve continuously.

The philosophy governing the software should remain stable.

---

# Golden Rule

No software implementation may contradict the Constitution or the Credit Doctrines.

Technology must always serve institutional principles.
