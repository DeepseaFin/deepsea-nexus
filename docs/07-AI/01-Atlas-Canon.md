# Atlas Canon

## Purpose

Defines the foundational principles and constitution for all Atlas decision systems, establishing core values, decision frameworks, operational constraints, and governance standards that guide all AI reasoning and recommendations.

## Business Value

Provides a consistent ethical and operational foundation that ensures all ATLAS decisions align with institutional values, regulatory requirements, and risk management objectives.

## Inputs

- Regulatory frameworks and requirements
- Institutional policies and procedures
- Risk management standards
- Stakeholder values and constraints

## Outputs

- Canonical principles document
- Decision-making constitution
- Constraint definitions
- Governance rules

## Dependencies

- Legal and compliance teams
- Risk management frameworks
- Regulatory advisory inputs

## Intelligence Workflow

The Atlas Canon serves as the reference layer for all downstream intelligence engines, ensuring consistent application of principles across Document, Legal, Credit, Fraud, and Regulatory engines.

## Future Enhancements

- Dynamic principle versioning
- Temporal constraint management
- Principle impact analysis tools
