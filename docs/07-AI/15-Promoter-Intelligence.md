# Promoter Intelligence Engine

Version: 1.0 (Concept)

Status: Foundational Philosophy

Owner: Deepsea Nexus

Classification: Internal Intellectual Property

---

# Purpose

The Promoter Intelligence Engine evaluates the credibility, integrity, governance, experience, and long-term reliability of the individuals responsible for the transaction.

Deepsea believes businesses are ultimately managed by people.

Therefore understanding the people behind a transaction is as important as understanding the financial statements.

The objective is not to judge individuals.

The objective is to understand institutional risk.

---

# Philosophy

A company may change.

Financial statements change every year.

Assets change.

Markets change.

Leadership character changes much more slowly.

ATLAS therefore evaluates both the company and the individuals responsible for leading it.

---

# Evaluation Areas

The engine may evaluate:

• Identity Verification

• Shareholding Structure

• Ultimate Beneficial Ownership (UBO)

• Director History

• Previous Companies

• Industry Experience

• Regulatory Records

• Litigation History

• Insolvency History

• Credit Bureau Information (where legally available and authorised)

• Previous Banking Relationships

• Previous Funding Behaviour

• Reputation

• Adverse Media

• Sanctions Screening

• Politically Exposed Person (PEP) Screening

• Internal Deepsea Experience

---

# Institutional Memory

The Promoter Intelligence Engine should continuously learn from Deepsea's own experience.

Examples:

• Previous transactions

• Timely repayments

• Defaults

• Delayed payments

• Cooperation during due diligence

• Covenant compliance

• Transparency

• Communication quality

This internal knowledge should gradually become more valuable than external data sources.

---

# Jurisdiction Awareness

Every jurisdiction provides different public information.

ATLAS shall use authorised information sources appropriate to each jurisdiction.

Examples include:

India

• CIBIL (where authorised)

• MCA

• Director records

• Insolvency records

UAE

• AECB (where authorised)

• Trade Licence

• UBO records

• Court records

Singapore

• Credit Bureau Singapore

• ACRA

United Kingdom

• Companies House

• Insolvency Service

Additional jurisdictions shall be added over time.

---

# Explainability

Every Promoter Intelligence recommendation shall explain:

• Why confidence increased.

• Why confidence decreased.

• Which evidence influenced the assessment.

No recommendation shall rely upon unexplained assumptions.

---

# Constitutional Principle

ATLAS evaluates evidence.

It does not stereotype individuals.

The purpose is to improve decision quality while respecting fairness, privacy, applicable laws, and due process.

Human approval always remains the final authority.

---

# Future Vision

Over time, the Promoter Intelligence Engine should become one of Deepsea's most valuable proprietary assets.

It will combine authorised external information with Deepsea's own institutional experience to produce an increasingly reliable understanding of promoter credibility.
