# Editorial Standard

## Purpose

Define editorial quality controls for formatting, consistency, and publication readiness across the Deepsea Legacy Repository.

## Scope

Applies to all markdown documents under docs/legacy, including standards, templates, and registry artifacts.

## Responsibilities

- Editorial Steward
  - Perform final editorial pass and release recommendation.
- Document Owner
  - Resolve editorial findings before approval.
- Domain Custodian
  - Confirm editorial closure prior to publication.

## Process

1. Structural checks
- Heading hierarchy is logical and complete.
- Required sections are present and ordered.

2. Language checks
- Tone is professional and institutional.
- Sentences are concise and unambiguous.

3. Formatting checks
- Lists and tables render correctly.
- Internal links resolve correctly.
- Terminology matches Deepsea Dictionary conventions.

4. Publication checks
- Metadata is complete and current.
- Version, status, and ownership align with registry.

## Examples

- Acceptable correction
  - Replacing informal language with accountable language.

- Acceptable correction
  - Converting mixed heading levels into consistent hierarchy.

- Blocking issue
  - Missing owner or missing effective date metadata.

## Future Evolution

Editorial maturity roadmap:
- Repository lint checks for markdown and heading discipline
- Terminology validation against controlled vocabulary lists
- Domain-specific editorial rulepacks for high-risk policy content
