# Deepsea Legacy Repository Governance

## Purpose

Define the governing model for the Deepsea Legacy Repository so institutional knowledge remains accurate, auditable, and durable across leadership, team, and technology transitions.

## Scope

This governance applies to all content and metadata under docs/legacy, including charters, policies, manuals, standards, decision records, lesson logs, mistake logs, research notes, and template-driven publications.

## Responsibilities

- Repository Custodian Council
  - Own governance policy, arbitration, and annual governance review.
- Domain Custodian
  - Own content quality and relevance for an assigned legacy domain.
- Document Owner
  - Accountable for accuracy, timeliness, and review readiness of a document.
- Reviewer
  - Validate factual integrity, policy alignment, and publication clarity.
- Editorial Steward
  - Enforce writing quality, formatting consistency, and DNOS tone.

## Process

1. Define ownership
- Every document must have a named owner and a domain custodian.

2. Author under standards
- All content must follow DOCUMENT-STANDARD, WRITING-STANDARD, and DOCUMENT-ID-STANDARD.

3. Review before publication
- Documents pass domain review and editorial review according to REVIEW-WORKFLOW.

4. Register and version
- Approved documents are recorded in registry/document-registry.md with their Document ID and version.

5. Change with traceability
- Material changes require a changelog entry and a clear rationale.

6. Periodic health check
- Each domain conducts a quarterly quality audit for staleness, conflicts, and missing ownership.

## Examples

- A policy update that changes approval authority requires:
  - Version increment
  - Review sign-off by domain custodian
  - Registry update
  - Changelog entry with impact summary

- A historical decision note can be added without policy impact if:
  - It follows template and metadata rules
  - It is reviewed for factual completeness
  - It is indexed in the registry

## Future Evolution

Governance will evolve toward:
- Automated quality gates for metadata completeness and style conformance
- Domain-level service level objectives for review turnaround
- Cross-domain dependency mapping to detect conflicting standards
- Custodian succession protocols for institutional continuity
