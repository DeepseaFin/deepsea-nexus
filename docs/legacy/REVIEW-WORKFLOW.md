# Review Workflow

## Purpose

Establish a predictable review and approval lifecycle for legacy documents so publication quality does not depend on individual preferences.

## Scope

Applies to new documents, revisions, and retirement notices under docs/legacy.

## Responsibilities

- Document Owner
  - Draft document, gather evidence, and address review findings.
- Domain Reviewer
  - Validate factual and operational correctness.
- Editorial Steward
  - Validate writing, structure, and publication compliance.
- Domain Custodian
  - Approve or reject publication.

## Process

1. Draft
- Owner prepares content from approved template and metadata standard.

2. Domain review
- Domain reviewer checks policy alignment, procedural feasibility, and evidence sufficiency.

3. Editorial review
- Editorial steward verifies style, clarity, and documentation consistency.

4. Approval
- Domain custodian signs off for publication readiness.

5. Publish and register
- Document is merged, registry updated, and changelog entry added.

Service expectations:
- Domain review target: two business days
- Editorial review target: one business day
- Final approval target: one business day

## Examples

- Standard policy update path:
  - Draft to domain review to editorial review to custodian approval to publication

- Fast-track correction path:
  - Editorial-only patch for non-behavioral wording fixes with PATCH version increment

## Future Evolution

Workflow enhancements under consideration:
- Automated reviewer assignment by domain
- Review aging dashboards and escalation triggers
- Approval evidence attachment requirements for major policy changes
